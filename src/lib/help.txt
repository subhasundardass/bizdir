SEO
==================
1. Ground rules
Title: aim for 50-60 characters. Google truncates by pixel width, not characters.
Description: aim for 120-155 characters.
Unique per URL. Never use the same title on two pages.
Put the main keyword first, brand last. Drop the brand if the title gets too long.
Use real data (counts, ratings, area), not filler.
Google may rewrite your title or description anyway. That's normal, and it's why good on-page content matters too.
